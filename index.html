About the contest

What is ICPC?

The International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) is the oldest, largest, and most prestigious university programming competition in the world. Teams of three students, with a single computer, have five hours to solve as many algorithmic problems as possible: data structures, math, graphs, and pure logic under pressure.

01

Local qualifier

University tournaments and training sessions to form teams.

02

Latin American Regional

Uruguay competes alongside Argentina and Paraguay at the South America South site.

03

Continental stage

The region’s best teams advance to an ICPC continental stage.

04

World Finals

The goal: a Uruguayan team competing among the best in the world.

The initiative

A house for Uruguay to reach its first World Finals

ICPC House is a coder house: we bring Uruguay’s top competitive programmers together under one roof to train full-time through November 7th. Nothing replaces hours of team practice, solving problems together, and living alongside other high-level competitors — that’s exactly what ICPC House aims to create.

The goal is concrete: bring a Uruguayan team to an ICPC World Finals for the first time.

Track record

Uruguay at the Latin American Regional

Competitive programming has been growing in Uruguay alongside its universities. Every year, Uruguayan teams compete at the ICPC Latin American Regional, gaining experience and strong results that point the way toward qualifying for a World Finals.

2025

A team from Universidad ORT Uruguay stood out at the ICPC Latin American Regional, finishing among the top spots in the regional ranking against hundreds of teams from across the continent.
